# Pocket Tank — ArkOS community port

This community port and installer for 64-bit ARM ArkOS was prepared by **KYordan**.

- Profile: https://github.com/KYordan
- Upstream project: https://github.com/mediacutlet/pocket-tank
- Target: R36S / RG351MP-class ArkOS handhelds running the SDL2 simulator
- Controls: D-pad or left stick to move; A or left-stick click to select; hold Start + Select to save and exit

This is an unofficial community port. It is not produced, maintained, or endorsed by the upstream Pocket Tank author. Upstream Pocket Tank copyright and license notices are included in `LICENSE`; third-party notices are in `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt`.
